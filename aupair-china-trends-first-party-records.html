Aupair China Trends: Five Findings from First-Party Records

The median age stayed at 22, while the China-intent applicant pool became more global and candidates began describing their cultural-exchange goals more clearly.

224historical applicant records, 2015–2019
125current public profiles with China intent
22median age in both observation windows

1. Age is the stable centre

Among profiles with a known age, 18–26-year-olds account for 84.2% of the historical group and 79.7% of the current group. Aupair China programs still fit a transition point: university, graduation, a gap year, or the beginning of a career.

2. The source pool is more global

Europe represents 62.5% of the historical cohort and 36.8% of the current China-intent cohort. The current pool also includes meaningful representation from Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America and the Caribbean, and the Middle East and North Africa.

Recruitment routes and website traffic influence these shares, so they are not a national census. They do show why nationality is a weak first filter compared with childcare evidence, availability, safety awareness, and communication.

3. Chinese majors remain a niche

Formal Chinese or China studies accounts for five historical candidates and six current candidates. Strong candidates can come from education, health, engineering, business, arts, languages, or social sciences.

4. Reported Chinese depth is more visible

Among records with comparable language information, intermediate-or-higher Chinese appears in 8 of 121 historical records and 37 of 81 current records. Different forms and missing-data rates make this directional evidence, not a precise national growth rate.

5. Candidates explain the purpose more clearly

Travel remains important, but current candidates more often describe what they hope to gain through family life: cultural exchange, childcare experience, immersion, belonging, and skills they can carry into their next stage.

Method and privacy boundary. This analysis uses de-identified records created through one program's real operations. Applications, public profiles, arrivals, and completed placements are separate layers. Six verified 2017 arrival records are only a small practical check. These findings are not official national placement statistics, and no names, contact details, identity documents, precise addresses, or identifiable family information are published.
